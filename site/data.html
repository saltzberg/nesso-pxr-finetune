Compound selection and cleaning

The working dataset has one PXR dose-response measurement for each of 4,134 compounds. Each compound has one chosen chemical form and one fixed place in the training plan.

What each row contains

  • A molecule: one cleaned structure linked to the original compound ID.
  • A measured value: pEC50 from the PXR dose-response assay.
  • An uncertainty: how precise the reported measurement is.
  • A quality score: a value that can give cleaner measurements more weight during training.
  • A role: development, the separate check set, or the final challenge set.

Chemical preparation

Each compound uses one prepared version of its structure. Other versions of the same compound are not counted as new measurements. The structure used by Nesso stays tied to the source compound ID.

How compounds are divided

The 3,344 development compounds are split into five groups of 668 or 669. Molecules with the same core chemical framework stay in the same group. Close chemical neighbors are also kept together. This makes the test groups less like the molecules used to train each model.

Data not used in the first model

Single-concentration measurements and the PXR-null counter assay are not part of the first training run. They remain available for later work. The 513 challenge compounds have no labels here and are not used to choose the model.
