# First-member upstream feasibility

Status: **completed** · Synthesis updated 2026-09-08. Original dates and immutable protocol history remain in the source evidence.

## Question

Can gradients reach a real upstream affinity module without changing the trunk?

## Design and fitted/frozen flow

Real prepared input → frozen trunk → first affinity member with injected adapters [engineering updates] → parity and saved-state replay.

## Results

Real-input gradient, frozen-weight, zero-update and adapter replay checks passed. This established a runnable intervention, not predictive superiority.

## Implication and limits

The first pilot used an engineering objective without assay labels and only one member.

All evidence is retrospective. Historical budgets, splits and raw versus uncertainty-weighted scores must remain distinct.
