Project summary · training complete

A trained PXR activity model from Nesso-1

This project uses Nesso-1 as a starting point for predicting PXR activity. Most of Nesso stays fixed. Three small prediction models have been trained on measured PXR data.

Where things stand

Training is complete. The three final models use all 4,134 labeled compounds. The PXR challenge compounds remain separate and are ready for prediction.

Work completed

  1. Use Nesso-1 to describe each molecule with two sets of 384 numbers.
  2. Train the two small prediction models on the PXR measurements.
  3. Choose training settings with five groups built from chemical structure.
  4. Check the chosen model once on the 790 compounds set aside for that purpose.
  5. Train three final models on all labeled compounds.
  6. Next: make predictions for the 513-compound challenge set.
