Scientific interpretation
- The X coordinate is exactly abs(fitted-truth)-abs(native-truth). Positive means worse, negative means improved, zero means equal absolute error (not necessarily unchanged prediction). This is not signed prediction bias or absolute prediction movement.
- Within these saved predictions, MW is negatively associated with absolute movement and positively associated with error change. Larger MW therefore tends toward less improvement/more worsening, not necessarily a positive error change for every large compound.
- For error change, raw MW rank correlations 0.304-0.357 attenuate to 0.050-0.142 after rank-potency, charge-sign and fold residualization. Adding rank assay SE changes these only slightly (0.054-0.145). Movement associations remain negative after adjustment (-0.335 to -0.163).
- Sparse overlap prevents clean attribution to MW rather than charge/potency: among 196 compounds below 200 Da, 194 have pEC50<4; the two with pEC50>=4 are charged. There are no small net-neutral compounds at pEC50>=4. Among small low-potency compounds, charged n=136 and net-neutral n=58.
- Within the low-potency strata both small charge groups have more negative mean error changes than their >=200 Da counterparts for all four method/budget combinations. This descriptive within-bin contrast does not control continuous potency, chemistry, charge magnitude, or interactions.
- Partial rank correlation here is Pearson correlation between residualized average ranks, with additive controls; it is not proof of a causal or fully independent MW effect. Missing overlap cannot be restored by residualization. No uncertainty intervals or significance claims were established.
- Prepared net formal charge is not experimental ionization at assay pH; net-neutral can include zwitterionic species. pEC50 is measured functional potency, not necessarily binding affinity.
- The 13376 rows are four repeated views of 3344 compounds, not 13376 independent compounds. Do not pool them for independent-observation inference. The analysis is retrospective, uses truth as a control, and does not establish a deployable correction.
- Weighted and unweighted summaries differ and some subgroup means change sign; specify which estimand is quoted. The threshold MW<200 and potency split <4 are descriptive bins, not validated mechanistic cutoffs.
