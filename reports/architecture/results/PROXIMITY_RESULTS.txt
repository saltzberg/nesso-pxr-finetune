Largest changes are not automatically best corrections. Of each arm's top20 native-relative changes, raw absolute error improves for 19/20 head-only and 17/20 LoRA at N100, 18/20 for both at N500. Among the top20 incremental LoRA changes, only 9/20 improve over head-only at N100 and 11/20 at N500; weighted error worsens by 0.377 and 0.090 respectively in those selected tails. Overall incremental LoRA still improves.
