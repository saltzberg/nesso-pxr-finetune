The most pronounced systematic pattern is that Nesso often regresses very weak
compounds toward roughly pEC50 4--5, whereas 2D more often resolves the low
tail. Nesso's wins tend to be cases where the 2D model makes a large isolated
error rather than a separate broad chemistry domain. This is consistent with
the high overall signed-residual correlations reported in
`SCIENTIFIC_REPORT.md`.
